## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-13-09 | Tampering | update_weights gamma value | mitigate | Service-layer reject of gamma < 0.5 before write; DB `CHECK (gamma >= 0.5)` (migration 0027) is the backstop — gamma=0 (farmable pure sum) is unreachable |
