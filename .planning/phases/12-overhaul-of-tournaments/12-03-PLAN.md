<objective>
Re-wire the service layer to the edition model: replace per-category `bootstrap_cycle` with a grid-snapped `bootstrap_edition` (D-13a — first/next edition snaps to the next grid boundary, never `now()`), move pause/debug setters to global config (D-03) with the production guard preserved (D-12 hiatus semantics), and collapse the outbox publisher to emit ONE combined `TournamentRolloverEvent` per rollover keyed by `edition_id` (D-09/D-10/D-11) while keeping reward/streak side-effects per child cycle.

Purpose: Make the API the sole producer of the single combined rollover event and the grid-aligned bootstrap, preserving the at-least-once publish-before-mark outbox guarantees and per-cycle reward correctness.

Output: Updated `tournament_service.py` + `tournament_outbox_service.py`, with service/outbox tests extended for grid-snap, hiatus, and the single combined event + idempotency.
</objective>
