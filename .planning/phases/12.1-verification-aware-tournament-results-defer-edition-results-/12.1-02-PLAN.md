Add the SDK contracts that the poller (Plan 04) and bot (Plan 05) build against, FIRST, so downstream
plans receive the exact struct shapes rather than discovering them. Adds the new results-only event

`msgspec.convert` cleanly or the row stays unpublished forever (Pitfall 2). This plan is interface-first:
it defines the wire contracts before anyone implements against them.
